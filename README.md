### 🚀 Selected work

**Physics-informed multimodal solar PV forecasting** (2026)
GRU weather branch plus a physics branch, fused by a regime-conditioned gate, with a clear-sky-index output head and 90% conformal prediction intervals. Benchmarked against 22 models over 5 seeds, 2 test years and 4 horizons: RMSE **39.69 ± 0.52 W/m²**, R² 0.977, **+15.9% skill** over smart persistence at 1 h. Best neural model, 4th of 22 overall, with 0.00% forecasts above the physical ceiling.

**[DriveGuard](https://github.com/shriman-08/DriveGuard)** (2026)
Real-time driver behaviour monitoring: detects phone use, drowsiness and looking away from a webcam, and streams violation events to a live multi-driver dashboard. *OpenCV · MediaPipe · YOLOv8 · Flask-SocketIO · ONNX*

**Wearable edge AI for knee rehabilitation** (2026)
Low-cost wearable that classifies five knee flexion angles with an int8 neural network on an ESP8266, cutting transmitted data by 99.99% versus raw cloud streaming. Paper accepted at ISTAS 2026. *Edge Impulse · TFLite · MPU6050 · Three.js*

**Physics-informed neural network for solar irradiance** (2025)
Domain physics embedded as loss regularisation across three PINN architectures: R² = 0.98, RMSE ≈ 40 W/m², about 15% better than baseline. *Python · TensorFlow · PyTorch*
